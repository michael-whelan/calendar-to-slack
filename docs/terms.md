## What the add-on does

It reads the guest list of a Google Calendar meeting you have open and creates a private
Slack channel containing the guests it can match to accounts in your Slack workspace. Where
your workspace forbids creating channels through the API, it opens a group DM instead.

Actions are performed as you, using a Slack token you authorise. Anything the add-on creates
is subject to your workspace's own policies and retention rules, and belongs to your
organisation, not to the maintainer.
